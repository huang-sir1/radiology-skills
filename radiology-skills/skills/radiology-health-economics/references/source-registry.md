## Currency and use rule

For HTA, coverage, reimbursement or procurement work, create a live source receipt for the exact
agency, programme, technology class, version and access date. If that cannot be done, retain
`STOP_REFERENCE_CASE_UNRESOLVED`. Do not silently substitute NICE, ISPOR, WHO or a published
country reference case for the responsible local authority.
