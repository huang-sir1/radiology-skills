# Experiment failure and decision log

conditions and experiments; do not overwrite them with the selected condition.

## Close-out

- Was the failure technical, intervention-related, model-context related, or evidence against the
  hypothesis? State uncertainty:
- Which competing explanations remain?
- What claim survives now?
- What is the single next decision-bearing action?
- Which failures/deviations must appear in Results, Supplement or Limitations?
