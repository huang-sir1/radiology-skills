# Local reviewer and revision experience registry

Use this registry to turn the team's real decision letters, reviewer reports, response actions and
revision outcomes into reusable, traceable practice knowledge. De-identify confidential material
and comply with the journal's and authors' sharing restrictions.

## Evidence and inference rule

- Registry ID/version/owner/date: [LOCAL_INPUT_NEEDED]
- Evidence classes: `SOURCE_DOCUMENT_VERIFIED`, `TEAM_RECOLLECTION_CONFIRMED`,
  `PARTLY_VERIFIED`, `NOT_ASSESSABLE`, `SUPERSEDED`
- A row may directly support qualitative guidance, a review checklist, a response pattern or a
  learner example within its recorded scope.
- Label separately: `FORMAL_JOURNAL_REQUIREMENT`, `RECURRENT_EMPIRICAL_PATTERN`, or
  `CASE_DERIVED_EXAMPLE`. A reviewer request is not automatically a journal rule.
- Do not add a generic selection-bias disclaimer to every use. State the denominator boundary only
  when making claims about frequency, general rejection causes, reviewer prevalence or acceptance
  probability across all submissions.
- Publication or acceptance after revision does not prove that one response action caused the
  outcome, nor that every scientific issue was resolved.

## Learner-use prompt

When using a lesson for mentoring, ask: Which part is a formal rule, which is a case-derived
expectation, what evidence would close it in this learner's study, what is the cheapest valid repair,
and what claim remains if the work is infeasible?
