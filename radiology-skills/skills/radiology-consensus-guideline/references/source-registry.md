# Authoritative source registry

These sources support the stable development framework. Verify current organizational rules and
product-specific authority at use. `Accessed` is the release verification date, not a permanent
currency claim.

| Authority/source | URL | Version/status | Accessed | Claim use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WHO Handbook for Guideline Development | https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241548960 | official second edition, 2014, with linked additional chapters | 2026-08-23 | scope, panel, COI, evidence, recommendation, stakeholder and publication process |
| GRADE EtD for clinical practice guidelines | https://www.bmj.com/content/353/bmj.i2089 | peer-reviewed primary methods paper, BMJ 2016;353:i2089 | 2026-08-23 | structured judgments for effects, certainty, values, resources, equity, acceptability and feasibility |
| NICE Developing Guidelines Manual | https://www.nice.org.uk/process/pmg20 | official manual, last updated 23 October 2025 | 2026-08-23 | committee, evidence review, economics, consultation, implementation, surveillance and updating |
| RAND/UCLA Appropriateness Method User's Manual | https://www.rand.org/pubs/monograph_reports/MR1269.html | RAND monograph MR-1269-DG-XII/RE, 2001 | 2026-08-23 | structured evidence-plus-panel appropriateness method and disagreement handling |
| CREDES Delphi guidance | https://doi.org/10.1177/0269216317690685 | peer-reviewed methodological guidance, 2017 | 2026-08-23 | prespecified Delphi conduct, stopping, consensus and transparent reporting |
| ACCORD consensus reporting guideline | https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1004326 | peer-reviewed reporting guideline, PLOS Medicine 2024; full text and stated scope inspected | 2026-09-04 | reporting across consensus purposes, including priority-setting; it is not a method-selection mandate or a universal GRADE requirement |
| ACR Practice Parameters and Technical Standards | https://www.acr.org/Clinical-Resources/Clinical-Tools-and-Reference/Practice-Parameters-and-Technical-Standards | official ACR live program page | 2026-08-23 | radiology product distinction, evidence/consensus drafting, field review, approval and revision context |
| ACR Appropriateness Criteria Rating Process | https://cs.acr.org/-/media/ACR/Files/Appropriateness-Criteria/Rating-Round-Information.pdf | official ACR process document, revised April 2021 | 2026-08-23 | anonymized rounds, median/disagreement handling and scenario-specific rating context |

These sources do not authorize the skill to use an organization's name, claim endorsement, impose a
legal standard, make individual patient decisions or bypass current local/society governance.
