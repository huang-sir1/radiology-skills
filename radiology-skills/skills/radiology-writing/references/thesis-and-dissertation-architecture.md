# Thesis and dissertation architecture for imaging research

Use this reference for a monograph thesis, article-based/cumulative thesis, chapter drafting or
revision, thesis-to-paper conversion and whole-thesis argument audit. Defense-slide production stays
with `radiology-paper2ppt`; institutional examination stays with the authorized graduate program.

## 1. Freeze the thesis passport

Record `institution/program/degree | thesis route | language | candidate/submission stage | official
handbook/template URL/version/date/accessed | chapter/order/length rules | published-manuscript reuse |
copyright/permissions | coauthor contribution declarations | ethics/data/AI disclosures | embargo/
repository | examiner/committee instructions | unresolved local rule`.

Use `LIVE_LOCAL_RULE_UNVERIFIED` until the current graduate-school/program source is checked. A prior
student's thesis, supervisor memory or journal format is an exemplar, not authority.

## 2. Choose the document route

| Route | Core architecture | Special risk |
|---|---|---|
| monograph | one problem/argument developed across integrated chapters | duplicated introductions/methods and disconnected study chapters |
| article-based/cumulative | framing synthesis + included papers/chapters + integrative discussion | copied publication text, inconsistent versions, coauthor/copyright/reuse and weak candidate synthesis |
| chapter mode | one chapter written against the whole-thesis spine | locally coherent chapter that contradicts cohort, values or claims elsewhere |
| thesis-to-paper | select one paper-sized contribution from the thesis evidence | compressing away required methods/limitations or claiming the whole thesis as one study |

## 3. Build the thesis argument spine

Freeze:

`field problem -> precise gap -> overarching question/objectives -> study/chapter contribution map ->
shared cohort/data/measurement and differences -> methods/analyses -> chapter claims/evidence ->
cross-study synthesis/discordance -> limitations/negative results -> original contribution -> future
work bounded by evidence`.

Every empirical chapter declares population, independent unit, imaging acquisition/measurement
passport, endpoint/estimand, analysis version and claim ceiling. Shared cohorts/data are not presented
as independent replications; repeated text/numbers resolve to canonical evidence and source locations.

## 4. Article-based and publication integrity

For each included/submitted/published manuscript record citation/DOI/status, exact accepted/published
version, candidate and coauthor contributions, reuse permission/licence, overlap with other chapters,
journal corrections/retractions and what bridging text was newly written. Do not silently paste a
publisher PDF or reuse figures without verified rights.

The integrative chapters must do more than concatenate abstracts: explain the common question,
methodological dependencies, convergent/discordant evidence, negative results, limitations and the
candidate's original synthesis.

## 5. Whole-thesis consistency and defense boundary

Audit title/objectives, terminology, cohort flow, sample/event counts, model/version names, values/CIs,
figures/tables, registration/ethics IDs, contribution claims and conclusions across every chapter and
appendix. Preserve meaningful null/failed studies and explain how they changed the program; do not
manufacture a linear success story.

Create a defense claim bank only from frozen thesis evidence: contribution, strongest evidence,
largest limitation, alternative explanation, reproducibility/translation boundary and likely
committee questions. Hand that bank to `radiology-paper2ppt`; rendering a defense deck does not close
thesis scientific or institutional gates.

## 6. Output and stops

Return `thesis passport -> route and chapter map -> overarching argument spine -> chapter claim/evidence
crosswalk -> shared-data/dependence map -> publication/reuse/contribution ledger -> consistency and
negative-result audit -> defense claim bank -> local-rule unresolved items`.

Stop a submission-readiness claim when local rules are unverified, included-paper permissions/
versions/contributions are unresolved, chapter results conflict, required ethics/data/AI disclosures
are missing, or the candidate's original contribution cannot be distinguished from coauthored work.
