## Evidence-state statement

Choose one and explain:

- `LOCAL_EXPERT_CONFIRMED`: confirmed by the named responsible role for this local context.
- `CASE_DERIVED`: observed in one or more defined local cases; not a prevalence estimate.
- `SOURCE_VERIFIED`: independently supported by a cited current or stable source.
- `AUTHOR_REPORTED`: supplied by the research team but not independently checked.
- `NOT_ASSESSABLE`: insufficient evidence or access.
