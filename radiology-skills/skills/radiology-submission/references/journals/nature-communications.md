at final audit.

## Route facts

- Initial format is flexible. Text and figures may be combined into one Word, TeX/LaTeX or PDF file
  up to 30 MB. Supplementary Information remains a separate file, preferably Word.
- Initial package includes cover letter, manuscript text, figures and conditional Supplementary
  Information. The cover letter supplies corresponding-author contact, significance/fit, reviewer
  suggestions or exclusions and prior editorial discussion.
- If a related manuscript with overlapping authors is under consideration or in press elsewhere,
  include its copy. Because the public guide does not close the upload extension, an active copy
  remains blocked until the exact NCOM portal designation is captured; an explicit N/A rationale is
  required when no such related work exists.
- Article guide: title at most 15 words; unreferenced abstract at most 200 words; main text suggested
  at most 5,000 words excluding abstract, Methods, references and legends; Methods normally below
  3,000 words; references suggested at most 70; legends at most 350 words.
- Main display items are capped at ten; manuscripts below 2,000 words are capped at four.
- Structure is Introduction, Results, Discussion, Methods. Discussion does not use subheadings.
- Final manuscript source is Word or TeX/LaTeX. The guide lists title page, author and affiliation
  details, text, references, acknowledgements when applicable, Author Contributions, Competing
  Interests, legends and tables. Word is double-spaced, single-column and page-numbered.
- Optional double-anonymized review is available; the author must anonymize all files and metadata and
  select the portal option.
- Relevant manuscripts entering review require Reporting Summary. All original research needs Data
  Availability; central custom code needs Code Availability. Source Data can be required for relevant
  papers but is not a universal initial attachment inferred from exemplars.
- Revision uses the decision link and requires a revised manuscript, change-cover letter, separate
  point-by-point response and individual figure uploads when figures are present. Do not promote the
  final-production high-resolution rule into every ordinary revision: the public workflow places
  production-quality final figures at final revision/acceptance. Clean/marked pairing and ordinary-
  revision accepted extensions remain exact-portal specific.

## File and material gates

| Stage | Material | Class | Publicly verified format/status |
|---|---|---|---|
| initial | combined manuscript/figures | required | Word, TeX/LaTeX or PDF; combined file up to 30 MB |
| initial | cover letter | required | extension must be refreshed from portal/guide |
| initial | Supplementary Information | conditional | separate; Word preferred; individual file ≤30 MB, total ≤150 MB |
| initial | related manuscript copy | conditional | overlapping-author work under consideration/in press; extension is an exact-portal check |
| final-files | line/vector figures | conditional | EPS, AI or PDF; Word/PPT also accepted in stated cases |
| final-files | photographic/raster figures | conditional | TIFF, RGB, at least 300 dpi |
| pre-review | Reporting Summary | conditional | relevant reporting branch |
| revision | revised manuscript + change-cover | required | distinct `clean` and `cover` roles in the executable contract |
| revision | point-by-point response | required | separate `response` role; exact extension is a portal check |
| revision | individual figures | conditional | one file per figure; ordinary-revision extensions are an exact-portal check |
| final-files | editable manuscript source | required | Word or TeX/LaTeX |

## Unverified current/portal gates

- Complete portal metadata and upload subtypes; Editorial Policy Checklist timing; universal Source
  Data timing; exact clean/marked revision pairing; ordinary-revision figure extensions. Keep
  unresolved decisive rules fail-closed.
