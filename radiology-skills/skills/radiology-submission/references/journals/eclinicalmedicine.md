## Route facts

- Single-anonymized review; title page in the manuscript carries author identity and contact details.
- Initial inventory includes cover, manuscript with tables/panels, figures, signed author statement
  form, DOI and funding statements, relevant in-press papers plus acceptance, applicable trial
  protocol/reporting material and Research in context for every primary research paper.
- Article main text is approximately 3,500–5,000 words with at most 30 references; the five-part
  semi-structured abstract is approximately 250 words. Qualitative/mixed-methods main text is 4,500
  words with at most 30 references.
- Research in context and Data sharing statement are required on the same evidence-disciplined basis
  as the journal's guide. Activate CONSORT, CONSORT-AI/SPIRIT-AI, STARD, STROBE, STREGA, PRISMA,
  GATHER, COREQ or SRQR by design.
- Relevant low- and middle-income country studies require the Equitable Partnership Declaration at
  revision, not as a universal initial-upload item.
- Supplement is a single PDF with contents and page numbers.
- Revision requires clean and highlighted manuscripts and detailed point-by-point response with the
  cover letter.
- The cover is entered under `Enter Comments` and abstract under `Submit Abstract`; capture all other
  required live fields from the actual portal.
