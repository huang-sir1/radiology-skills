## Route facts

- Single-anonymized review. Initial submission has no special format; PDF and Word are accepted, and
  TeX/LaTeX authors submit a compiled PDF initially and source after acceptance.
- Initial package includes cover letter, manuscript, figures, conditional SI and design checklists.
  The corresponding author supplies the mandatory signed competing-interests statement for all
  authors. Related under-review or in-press manuscripts require a copy and status as additional
  supporting information. SI is one separate file, preferably PDF.
- Article: title at most 15 words; unstructured abstract at most 150 words; no strict total word/page
  limit but concise writing required; 60 references stated with nonabsolute enforcement; legends at
  most 350 words.
- Structure: unheaded Introduction; Results may have subheads; Discussion has no subheads or separate
  Limitations/Conclusions; Methods may have subheads and all Methods stay in the main manuscript.
- Data Availability accompanies the manuscript; Code Availability is conditional. Relevant papers
  entering review require Reporting Summary. The initial Reporting Summary and Editorial Policy
  Checklist are encouraged, not automatically mandatory.
- For the journal's own checklist wording, CONSORT and PRISMA are required before peer review for
  matching designs; other named checklists are strongly encouraged unless a separate current policy
  establishes a stronger obligation. Oncology routing must still identify REMARK, STARD,
  TRIPOD/TRIPOD+AI or STROBE applicability; an AI label does not replace biomarker/model reporting.
- Revision needs the decision link, change-cover letter, separate response and revised text. The
  public high-resolution-figure production rule belongs to final revision/accepted-final files, not
  every ordinary revision. Clean/marked pairing and ordinary-revision figure mechanics remain exact-
  portal specific for machine closure; a decision letter is reviewed by a human and cannot be labeled
  `PORTAL_CURRENT`.

## File and material gates

| Stage | Material | Class | Publicly verified format/status |
|---|---|---|---|
| initial | manuscript | required | PDF or Word; compiled PDF for TeX/LaTeX |
| initial | cover letter | required | extension is a live portal check |
| initial | signed competing-interests statement | required | file versus portal mechanics must be captured live |
| initial | related manuscript copy/status | conditional | under-review or in-press related work |
| initial | Supplementary Information | conditional | one separate file, PDF preferred |
| pre-review | Reporting Summary | conditional | relevant life-science branch |
| pre-review | CONSORT or PRISMA checklist | conditional required | matching trial or systematic-review design |
| initial/pre-review | other oncology/design checklist | optional/recommended unless separately mandated | exact design determines the form and claim audit |
| revision | point-by-point response | required | separate; clean/marked pairing and extension are portal checks |
| final-files (publisher: accepted final) | editable source | required | Word or TeX/LaTeX source |
| final-files | production figures | conditional when figures exist | high-resolution file type/route must be resolved from current exact portal |

## Unverified current/portal gates

- Display-item cap, final figure resolution/extensions, table extension, universal Source Data timing,
  Editorial Policy Checklist timing and complete portal fields.
