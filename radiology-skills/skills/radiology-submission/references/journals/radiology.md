# Radiology — submission profile

Snapshot checked: 2026-08-22. Scope: `Original Research`. The official page records January and
March 2026 updates. This is a strict-format route, not a free-format initial submission.

## Authoritative routes

- `RAD-IFA`: [Radiology Publication Instructions for Authors](https://pubs.rsna.org/page/radiology/author-instructions)
- `RAD-STYLE`: [Scientific editing guidance](https://pubs.rsna.org/page/radiology/author-instructions/scientificediting)
- `RAD-POLICY`: [RSNA Journals Editorial Policies](https://pubs.rsna.org/page/policies)
- `RAD-ACK-FORM`: [Acknowledgment and Permission Form (DOCX)](https://pubs.rsna.org/pb-assets/Radiology/PIA/AcknowledgePermissionFormRY-1705075670100.docx)
- `RAD-PORTAL`: [Radiology ScholarOne](https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/rad) — current instance fields
  must be captured from the live submission.

## Route facts

- Double-anonymized review. Audit reviewer-facing manuscript, figures, tables, supplement, checklists,
  filenames, comments, tracked-change authors and metadata. Keep editor-only identity materials—full
  title page, cover-letter content and acknowledgements/permissions—on their designated routes rather
  than anonymizing them. Do not remove formal study/trial names, institution-free IRB numbers, product
  names or references when the guide allows them.
- Text files are `.docx`; `.doc` is not accepted from January 2026. The anonymous main manuscript is
  one DOCX containing abbreviated title page, structured abstract, main text, references and figure
  legends; editable tables sit at the end, one per page. Do not add manual page numbers.
- A separate full title-page DOCX includes title at most 15 words, author/degrees/affiliations,
  originating-institution address, corresponding contact, funding, article type, main-text word count
  and Data Sharing Statement.
- Required cover-letter content includes title, full author list, applicable co-first-author rationale,
  overlap/related manuscripts, conflicts/industry support and exclusive-submission confirmation. The
  current route permits pasting it in the Cover Letter field or using an attachment; an attachment's
  accepted extension is a live-portal check, not a fixed DOCX rule.
- Initial figures are all placed in one separate Word file, one figure per page followed by its legend;
  the legends also remain in the main manuscript. At revision, upload each figure/panel as an
  individual high-resolution file. The public guide supplies candidate format sets rather than one
  universally interchangeable extension list: raster imaging lists PSD/TIF/AI/EPS at at least 300 dpi
  and excludes PNG, while graphs/line art list TIFF/PPT/AI/EPS/native Word/Excel with a 1200-dpi target.
  The exact live revision-portal designation and accepted format resolve any conflict for that
  submission instance; do not award PASS from the public candidate union alone.
- Supplementary text/tables/figures and legends form one separate DOCX named as the Supplemental File
  for Review. Multimedia is uploaded separately; the current guide lists MPG/MPEG/MOV/AVI/WMV/MP4,
  GIF and MP3, with MP4/MP3 preferred and single files usually below 30 MB. Acknowledgements are a
  separate identified editor-only route. When acknowledged persons or permission-requiring material
  exists, use the journal-linked current DOCX acknowledgment/permission form rather than embedding it
  in the blinded main manuscript.
- Original Research: Introduction–Discussion at most 3,000 words; structured abstract at most 300;
  normally at most 35 references, six figures and four tables; Summary Statement one sentence at most
  30 words; up to three Key Results totalling at most 75 words; up to ten abbreviations.
- Abstract headings: Background, Purpose, Materials and Methods, Results, Conclusion. Human research
  uses a Figure 1 flow diagram and Table 1 baseline demographics/clinical characteristics.
- Submit the applicable editable reporting checklist (for example STARD, CONSORT, PRISMA-DTA or
  STROBE). Data sharing is on the full title page; an independent Source Data file is not universal.
- Revision adds clean and marked anonymous main manuscripts, point-by-point response and individual
  high-resolution figures. Word revision author metadata is changed to `Author`.

## Stage material matrix

| Stage | Material | Class | Publicly verified format/status |
|---|---|---|---|
| initial | anonymous main manuscript | required | DOCX only |
| initial | full title page | required | separate DOCX |
| initial | cover-letter content | required one-of | paste into portal field or attach; attachment type is a live check |
| initial | combined figure review file | required when figures exist | one Word file, one figure/page plus legend |
| initial | tables | conditional | editable Word tables at main-manuscript end, not separate images/files |
| initial | acknowledgements/permissions | conditional | journal-linked separate DOCX form; identified editor-only material |
| initial | reporting checklist | conditional | applicable official Word checklist |
| initial | supplemental text/tables/figures | conditional | one separate DOCX with legends |
| initial | supplemental multimedia | conditional | each media file separate; use guide-listed MPG/MPEG/MOV/AVI/WMV/MP4/GIF/MP3 route |
| revision | clean anonymous manuscript | required | DOCX |
| revision | marked anonymous manuscript | required | DOCX; metadata author `Author` |
| revision | response | required | point-by-point; portal file type live-check |
| revision | individual revision figures | conditional | one figure/panel per file; public-guide candidate formats only, with exact accepted type resolved in the live revision portal |

## Portal-only checks

- Live Step 1–7 metadata, ORCID, Summary Statement, figure/table counts, applicable code link/details,
  generated HTML/PDF proof and electronic contribution/copyright workflows. These are portal fields or
  forms, not fabricated physical upload files.
- Revision figure guidance currently conflicts on whether AI is accepted in every upload path
  (manuscript-organization text versus Step 2 list). Treat all public-guide revision extensions as
  candidate formats and resolve the actual designation and accepted format from the exact live
  revision portal; do not upgrade any candidate to PASS solely from one conflicting subsection.
