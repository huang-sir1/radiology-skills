# Nature Medicine — submission profile

Snapshot checked: 2026-08-22. Scope: `Article`; verify other content types independently.
The official pages do not show a visible revision date. Refresh them during every final audit.

## Route facts

- Review model: optional double-anonymized. The author must remove identity from every supplied file
  and its metadata and select the portal option; the cover letter carries author contact details.
- Initial submission is format-flexible. PDF, Word and TeX/LaTeX are accepted; TeX/LaTeX authors
  provide a compiled PDF. The main package contains Methods, figures and applicable Extended Data;
  cover letter is separate and Supplementary Information is conditional.
- The cover letter explains significance and fit, related manuscripts and prior editor contact. It
  may recommend or exclude reviewers and is not shown to reviewers.
- Article limits: abstract at most 150 words and unreferenced; main text at most 4,000 words excluding
  abstract, Methods, references and legends; at most six main display items; typically at most 60
  references; at most ten Extended Data items.
- Structure: unheaded introduction, Results, Discussion, Online Methods. Discussion has no subheads.
- Life-science/clinical manuscripts entering review require the relevant Reporting Summary. All
  original research needs a Data Availability statement; central custom code needs a separate Code
  Availability statement. A universal initial-upload `Source Data` file is not established.
- Nature Medicine's clinical-AI page says medical-AI studies must follow MI-CLAIM-GEN and provide its
  checklist. Trial branches additionally activate the current clinical-trial protocol/SAP and
  applicable CONSORT/CONSORT-AI duties.
- Revision uses the decision link and needs a revised manuscript, separate point-by-point response and
  a cover letter describing changes. Public guidance does not establish a universal clean-plus-marked
  file pair or its extensions; human-review the decision letter, but use only the exact live portal or
  a validated contract update for deterministic closure.
- Accepted final manuscript must provide editable Word or TeX/LaTeX source; a PDF alone is not an
  editable production source.

## File and material gates

| Stage | Material | Class | Publicly verified format/status |
|---|---|---|---|
| initial | manuscript | required | PDF, Word or compiled TeX/LaTeX PDF |
| initial | cover letter | required | exact extension must be refreshed from guide/portal |
| initial | Supplementary Information | conditional | normally one PDF for text/simple tables; complex data may use workbook/CSV |
| pre-review | Reporting Summary | conditional | current official form; applicable life-science/clinical branch |
| initial/pre-review | MI-CLAIM-GEN checklist | conditional | medical-AI branch |
| revision | point-by-point response | required | separate file; exact portal closes mechanics, while a decision letter is human-adjudicated |
| final-files (publisher: accepted final) | editable manuscript source | required | Word or TeX/LaTeX source; PDF alone is insufficient |

## Unverified current/portal gates

- Complete upload designations and author-entered fields; Editorial Policy Checklist timing; universal
  clean/marked convention; Article title and legend hard limits. Any decisive instance remains
  `PORTAL_CHECK` or `UNVERIFIED_CURRENT`, never PASS by analogy.
