## Route facts

- Single-anonymized review. No current journal-page evidence supports an optional double-anonymized
  route.
- Initial submission has no special format. PDF and Word are accepted; TeX/LaTeX authors supply a
  compiled PDF initially and editable source after acceptance. The guide notes reviewer preference for
  embedded figures. Resolve the apparent editable-file versus initial-PDF wording by stage, not by
  declaring PDF invalid.
- Initial package includes cover letter, manuscript, figures, conditional Supplementary Information
  and study-design checklists. The corresponding author also supplies the mandatory signed competing-
  interests statement for all authors. If a related manuscript is under review or in press, supply its
  copy and status as additional supporting information. SI is one separate file, preferably PDF.
- Article: title at most 15 words; unstructured abstract at most 150 words; no strict total word/page
  limit but concise writing required; 60 references stated with nonabsolute enforcement; legends at
  most 350 words.
- Structure: unheaded Introduction; Results may have subheads; Discussion has no subheads and no
  separate Limitations or Conclusions; Methods may have subheads and all Methods remain in the main
  manuscript rather than SI.
- Data Availability is included with the manuscript; Code Availability is added when applicable.
  Relevant life-science papers entering review require Reporting Summary. The journal encourages the
  Reporting Summary and Editorial Policy Checklist at initial submission; encouragement is not an
  initial-upload hard requirement. For design checklists, CONSORT and PRISMA are required before peer
  review for matching designs; STROBE, CARE, COREQ/SRQR, STARD/TRIPOD, CHEERS and ARRIVE are strongly
  encouraged on the journal page unless a separately verified current policy makes one mandatory.
- Revision requires decision-link submission, a cover letter describing changes, separate response
  and revised text. The public high-resolution-figure production rule belongs to final
  revision/accepted-final files, not every ordinary revision upload. Tracked changes must be absent
  from the final manuscript; ordinary-revision figure mechanics remain an exact-portal question.

## File and material gates

| Stage | Material | Class | Publicly verified format/status |
|---|---|---|---|
| initial | manuscript | required | PDF or Word; compiled PDF for TeX/LaTeX |
| initial | cover letter | required | exact extension is a live portal check |
| initial | signed competing-interests statement | required | file versus portal mechanics must be captured live |
| initial | related manuscript copy/status | conditional | under-review or in-press related work |
| initial | Supplementary Information | conditional | one separate file, PDF preferred |
| pre-review | Reporting Summary | conditional | applicable life-science branch |
| pre-review | CONSORT or PRISMA checklist | conditional required | matching trial or systematic-review design |
| initial/pre-review | other design checklist | optional/recommended unless separately mandated | preserve the journal's stronger-versus-encouraged wording |
| revision | point-by-point response | required | separate; extension and clean/marked pairing are portal checks |
| final-files (publisher: accepted final) | editable source | required | Word or TeX/LaTeX source |
| final-files | production figures | conditional when figures exist | high-resolution file type/route must be resolved from current exact portal |

## Unverified current/portal gates

- Main-display cap, final figure resolution/extensions, table extension, universal Source Data upload,
  Editorial Policy Checklist timing, clean/marked revision pair and full portal fields.
