# Imaging-only submission and rebuttal package

Use this module only when imaging, radiomics, deep imaging or an imaging-derived endpoint is the study
scope and no molecular mechanism layer is claimed. Do not request omics accession numbers,
sample-to-image mapping, tissue blocks or molecular validation simply because those items appear in a
radiogenomics checklist.

## Scientific readiness

- State the clinical question, target population, index imaging input, outcome and intended use.
- Show patient flow, exclusions, missingness and the independent validation cohort, if claimed.
- Report acquisition protocol, scanner or site variation, reconstruction and preprocessing.
- Describe segmentation or annotation, reader expertise, blinding and reproducibility.
- Provide feature extraction or deep-model specification, software versions and frozen parameters.
- Demonstrate patient-level splitting, leakage control, feature-selection nesting and calibration.
- Report effect sizes or performance with uncertainty, comparator models and failure analyses.
- Separate association, diagnostic accuracy, prognostic prediction, treatment benefit and clinical
  utility; each needs a different estimand and validation argument.

## Supplement and reproducibility package

- Patient flow diagram and cohort table.
- Imaging protocol and scanner table by site or cohort.
- Segmentation or reader reproducibility analysis.
- IBSI-compatible radiomics settings or a complete deep-feature/model specification.
- Feature filtering, model coefficients or executable model artifact where permitted.
- Full cross-validation, external validation, calibration and decision-utility outputs as applicable.
- Sensitivity analyses for site, scanner, protocol, tumour volume and missingness.
- Data, feature and code availability statements with honest privacy restrictions.
- Appropriate reporting checklist such as CLEAR, IBSI, TRIPOD+AI, STARD-AI, CLAIM or STROBE,
  selected by the actual design rather than attached indiscriminately.

## Rebuttal constraints

For every reviewer point, use a frozen finding ID and report: concern, action, evidence location,
residual limitation and revised claim. Do not answer requests for molecular mechanism by inventing or
implying absent omics data. Either add a justified analysis within the authorized data or lower the
claim to imaging association or prediction.

Before upload verify: author approval, de-identification, exact line and figure references, resolved
placeholders, stable model version, complete supplementary files, data/code restrictions and
consistency among abstract, Results, tables, captions and cover letter.
