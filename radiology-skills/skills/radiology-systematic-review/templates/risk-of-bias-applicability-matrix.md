| Route | Required decision-bearing fields |
|---|---|
| Diagnostic accuracy | selection/spectrum, index threshold/blinding, reference standard, flow/timing, patient-versus-lesion applicability |
| Prevalence/incidence | population/sampling frame, recruitment/non-response, state/event ascertainment, analyzed/eligible denominator or person-time, period, missingness and analysis/reporting |
| Observational association | selection, exposure definition/contrast, outcome, temporal order, confounding/adjustment class, missingness, selective analysis/reporting and applicability of the contrast |
| Reliability/agreement/method comparison | object/subject and reader/device selection, acquisition/measurement protocol, order/blinding, exact metric/model/form, repeat hierarchy, exclusions/missingness, dependence and intended-condition applicability |
