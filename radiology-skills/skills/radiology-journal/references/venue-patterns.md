# Venue publication patterns (verify current scope live)

Orientation only — match the paper to the pattern, then **confirm the venue's current aims and
recent comparable papers** with `radiology-search` before committing. Do not select on impact
factor.

## Tiers by what they reward

| Venue | Pattern to investigate | Evidence to inspect for the actual article type |
|---|---|---|
| **Nature Medicine** | Clinically transformative, often prospective/multi-cohort imaging AI | Strong external/prospective evidence; clinical readiness; fairness |
| **Lancet Digital Health / Lancet Oncology** | Clinical impact + generalisability | Multi-center, external or prospective validation; utility framing |
| **Radiology / Radiology: AI** | Rigorous, clinically framed imaging research | Claim-matched validation, appropriate uncertainty and reporting; calibration/DCA/reader evidence when relevant |
| **Nature Communications** | Methodological novelty + broad significance | Solid validation; convincing increment |
| **Cell Reports Medicine** | Translational + biological/clinical insight | Validation + mechanism or strong clinical relevance |
| **npj Digital Medicine / npj Precision Oncology** | Digital-health / precision-oncology angle | Validation + translational/biological relevance; open science |
| **eClinicalMedicine / eBioMedicine** | Clinically useful, translational | Clear question; adequate validation |
| **Strong specialty (e.g. EurRadiol, JNM, Med Image Anal for methods)** | Specialty/methods depth | Field-appropriate rigor; reproducibility |

## Tier-deciding signals (what moves a paper up)

The following signals concern prediction/clinical-use imaging manuscripts where the stated claim
activates them. They are not universal requirements for every article type or official venue rules.

- **External / prospective validation** present and honest.
- **Multi-center** with per-center results and harmonisation.
- **Reader/workflow or decision evidence**, matching the claim; reader accuracy or modeled net
  benefit does not by itself establish patient benefit.
- **Calibration** reported, not just discrimination.
- **Adequate sample size / events** for the claim.
- **Clear, defensible novelty** and biological grounding where relevant.
- **Reporting compliance** (CLAIM/CLEAR/TRIPOD+AI/IBSI).

## Tier-capping signals (what pulls it down)

- Single-center, single-scanner, retrospective only.
- Internal validation dressed as external.
- AUC only; no calibration/utility.
- Small n / few events; artificial 1:1 prevalence.
- Incremental method with no clinical or biological payoff.

## Venue legitimacy check (predatory screening)

Run this screen alongside the live scope verification before recommending any unfamiliar venue:

- **Solicitation pattern** — unsolicited flattering invitation emails or guaranteed-acceptance
  offers are warning signs.
- **Indexing** — verify claimed indexing in the relevant official registry. DOAJ applies to
  open-access journals; its absence is not evidence against a subscription venue. Distinguish
  PubMed discoverability from MEDLINE selection; Google Scholar alone is not quality assurance.
- **APC transparency** — fees stated openly on the journal site before submission; beware fees
  disclosed only after acceptance.
- **Editorial board and publisher** — real, reachable editors in the field; a publisher with a
  credible portfolio (COPE membership is a plus).
- **Peer-review timeline** — guaranteed acceptance or implausible promised full-review timelines
  deserve investigation. A fast desk decision or a reported median alone does not prove sham review.
- **Scope/editorial match** — inspect relevant editors and comparable work; legitimate
  multidisciplinary journals can publish both oncology and engineering.

Investigate warning signs in combination and describe what is established. Do not label a venue
predatory or reject it automatically from one non-diagnostic signal.

## Honest mapping rule

For a predictive imaging model claiming broad clinical deployment, single-center internal validation
usually weakens fit to top clinical venues. State the claim-specific reason and compare current
published work. Do not apply that heuristic as an exclusion rule to every retrospective, technical,
discovery, mechanism or rare-disease paper.
