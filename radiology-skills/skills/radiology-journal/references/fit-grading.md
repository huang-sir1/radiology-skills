# Fit grading — score the paper, then build the ladder

Grade the manuscript on design-relevant dimensions, explain material limitations, then map to
reach/target/safety as relative scope-fit options. These are advisory judgments, not validated
acceptance scores or universal journal cutoffs.

## Grade these dimensions

| Dimension | Weak | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| **Validation** | Evidence inadequate for the stated claim | Validation matched to measurement, prediction, accuracy, mechanism or deployment claim |
| **Centers / transport** | Site effects unexamined despite broad transport claims | Site/protocol variation assessed where the claim requires it |
| **Time / design** | Temporal design cannot answer the question | Prospective, retrospective or temporal design justified for the question; these are distinct |
| **Clinical utility** | Utility claimed from discrimination alone | Decision, reader/workflow or patient-outcome evidence matched to the claimed benefit |
| **Sample size / events** | Small / few events | Adequate for the claim |
| **Novelty** | Incremental | Clear, defensible, possibly mechanistic |
| **Reporting** | Gaps | CLAIM/CLEAR/TRIPOD+AI/IBSI compliant |

Use weak / moderate / strong / not-applicable with evidence. Explain the decisive limitation or
trade-offs; do not pretend one dimension determines a universal publication ceiling. Calibration
applies to predicted probabilities; DCA requires a defined clinical decision and defensible threshold
range; reader studies apply to human-use claims. A technical measurement/reconstruction or mechanistic
paper need not add these analyses solely to move up a journal tier.

## Map grade → ladder

- **Reach** — a more selective but plausible fit supported by an unusual contribution; unresolved
  validity defects cannot be offset by unrelated strengths.
- **Target** — a strong match to scope, article type and recent comparable work.
- **Safety** — a broader-fit or less selective alternative, with no implied acceptance or minor-
  revision promise. Verify scope, legitimacy and practical constraints just as carefully.

For each rung: **venue (verify live) · match reason · risk · turnaround consideration · the one
thing to strengthen to climb.**

## Strengthening choices (rank by the actual defect and cost)

1. Match **independent validation** to an unsupported transport claim → radiology-design.
2. Add **reader/workflow/patient-benefit evidence** when such benefit is claimed → radiology-translation.
3. Evaluate **calibration / decision-curve** only when the estimand and intended decision support it → radiology-stats.
4. Add **biological grounding** when biological inference is material → radiology-radiogenomics.
5. Close **reporting-guideline** gaps → radiology-reporting.

## Output skeleton

```
Paper profile:   [type | selling point | biggest weakness]
Fit grade:       [per-dimension + limiting factor]
Ladder:
  Reach:   [venue* | reason | risk | strengthen-to-climb]
  Target:  [venue* | reason | risk]
  Safety:  [venue* | reason]
Strengthen-first: [ordered levers]
Verify now:      [live scope checks → radiology-search]
(* current scope to be verified live)
```
