# Evidence states, preservation and escalation

Use this reference for triage and any institutional handoff. It governs language and process; it
does not import a particular jurisdiction's legal definition into another institution.

## 1. Keep three axes separate

| Axis | Allowed states | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Concern classification | `OBSERVATION`, `INTEGRITY_SIGNAL`, `ALLEGATION`, `INSTITUTIONAL_FINDING` | what kind of statement is being recorded |
| Source verification | `DOCUMENT_VERIFIED`, `AUTHOR_REPORTED`, `THIRD_PARTY_REPORTED`, `NOT_VERIFIED`, `CONFLICTING` | how the supporting record is known |
| Action | `CLARIFY`, `RECONSTRUCT_PROVENANCE`, `CORRECT`, `PRESERVE_AND_MONITOR`, `STOP_AND_ESCALATE` | what can safely happen next |

An `INTEGRITY_SIGNAL` can be serious without being an allegation. An `ALLEGATION` can exist without
supporting evidence. An `INSTITUTIONAL_FINDING` applies only to the scope and status of the inspected
decision record; do not generalize it to other people, projects or conduct.

## 2. Atomic concern record

Record:

`Concern ID | classification | neutral statement | artifact/version/location | source-verification
state | date/time | person or system that produced the source | alternative explanations | affected
Claim/Result/participant/public record | evidence needed | preservation action | next authorized
owner | confidentiality/access | current status`.

Avoid labels such as fraud, falsified, fabricated, plagiarized, manipulated, innocent or guilty
unless quoting an attributed allegation or document-verified institutional finding. Use neutral
descriptions such as “the two registered primary outcomes do not match” or “the source image for
panel B has not been located.”

## 3. Preservation rules

- Preserve originals, metadata, audit logs, hashes, version history and read-only copies where the
  user is authorized. Do not normalize filenames, resave images or rerun code over the only copy.
- Record custody and access without claiming forensic chain of custody unless an authorized process
  established it.
- Keep confidential evidence separate from public summaries. Minimize personal and patient data.
- Do not solicit covert access, impersonate an investigator, search private accounts or bypass
  institutional controls.
- If preservation requires an external or administrative action, stop at a factual recommendation
  until the user or authorized owner performs it.

## 4. Escalation ladder

| Condition | Minimum response | Typical authorized owner |
|---|---|---|
| missing source, explainable version mismatch, unclear contribution | preserve and clarify | project lead/data steward/corresponding author |
| material integrity signal with unresolved claim impact | freeze affected claim and prepare handoff | research-integrity officer or equivalent |
| allegation of fabrication, falsification or plagiarism | record as attributed allegation; do not investigate | institution's designated integrity process |
| active participant/animal/biosafety risk | stop affected activity and route immediately | `radiology-ethics` plus authorized local committee/office |
| PHI/security exposure | minimize handling and stop dissemination | privacy/security/data-protection owner |
| material published-record unreliability | preserve evidence and notify through authorized route | institution plus journal/publisher |
| retaliation, coercion or personal-safety concern | do not mediate or promise protection | institutional ombuds/integrity/HR/legal or emergency owner |

Local policy decides reporting duties, confidentiality, respondent/complainant rights, inquiry,
investigation, appeal and corrective action. Verify the current institutional process rather than
turning the ORI model or a publisher flowchart into universal law.

## 5. Stop conditions

Stop ordinary audit and return `STOP_AND_ESCALATE` when:

- the user asks the skill to declare misconduct or publicly identify an accused person;
- evidence alteration, deletion or destruction is requested or appears imminent;
- confidential evidence cannot be handled within the authorized channel;
- an affected result guides current participant care or safety;
- retaliation or coercion is reported;
- a formal allegation is already under institutional review; or
- a publisher/funder/institutional decision is needed before further dissemination.

Continue only with neutral preservation, scope mapping and authorized handoff support.
