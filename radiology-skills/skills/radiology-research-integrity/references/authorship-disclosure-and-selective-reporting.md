# Authorship, disclosures, AI use and selective reporting

Use this reference when the concern involves credit, accountability, sponsor influence, competing
interests, AI assistance or a mismatch between a frozen plan and the reported record.

## 1. Authorship and contribution are related but distinct

Build a versioned ledger for every named author and non-author contributor:

`person ID | affiliation | ICMJE authorship criteria status | CRediT roles with evidence | draft/review
and final-approval state | accountability acceptance | author/non-author/unknown | order rationale |
change history | dispute status | author confirmation`.

- CRediT records contribution roles; it does not by itself determine authorship or author order.
- Funding acquisition, supervision, data access, editing or status alone do not automatically earn
  authorship. Conversely, eligible contributors must not be denied the opportunity to participate in
  drafting, final approval and accountability merely to exclude them.
- Do not decide a dispute from hierarchy, email tone or contribution percentages. Preserve records
  and route unresolved authorship disputes through the institution or journal.
- Record local collaborators and data-originating teams; avoid invisible or extractive authorship.

## 2. COI and sponsor-role ledger

Record financial and non-financial relationships, patents/equity, consulting, expert testimony,
academic competition and institutional interests only from supplied disclosure records. Separately
record the sponsor's role in design, data access, analysis, interpretation, writing, publication
choice and approval.

`NO_CONFLICT_DECLARED` is an author's attestation, not proof that no conflict exists. A disclosed
relationship is not itself misconduct. The integrity task is transparent, version-matched disclosure
and protection of analytic/publication independence; the venue/institution decides management.

## 3. AI-assistance record

For writing, translation, coding, literature work, statistics, image generation/editing or figure
production record:

`tool/model/version if known | date | user | purpose | inputs and confidentiality authority | outputs
used | human verification | source/permission check | affected artifacts | venue/funder disclosure
location | unresolved policy check`.

- AI is not an author and cannot accept accountability.
- Humans remain responsible for accuracy, originality, citations, permissions, confidentiality and
  the submitted artifact.
- Do not upload unpublished manuscripts, peer-review material, identifiable imaging or controlled
  data to a tool without authorization and a compatible data-use route.
- Current journal, funder and institutional rules must be verified live. An advisory rule in this
  skill is not a submission clearance.

## 4. Selective-reporting audit

Reconcile protocol/preregistration/SAP and amendments against analysis logs, source results and every
public artifact. Check:

- primary/secondary outcomes, estimands, time points, subgroups and analysis populations;
- eligibility, exclusions, missing/non-evaluable cases and cohort-flow denominators;
- model, threshold, feature, ROI, series, slice, site, scanner and seed selection;
- protected-test access, repeated evaluation and unreported tuning/adaptation;
- null, negative, failed, subgroup-harm and external-validation results;
- deviations and result-aware changes; and
- abstract, figure, table, supplement, repository, preprint and conference-record consistency.

An undocumented difference is an `INTEGRITY_SIGNAL`; it is not proof of intent. Reconstruct timing
and decision context. If a result-aware change is scientifically justified, label it exploratory or
amended and report its effect rather than hiding it.

## 5. Output

Return the contribution/disclosure/AI ledgers, plan-to-report comparison, exact discrepancies,
alternative explanations, affected claims, author confirmations needed and any institution/venue
handoff. Do not resolve a dispute by editing the author list or disclosure form without explicit
author and institutional/venue authority.
